## D. Alignment verdict — does the strategy fit THIS buyer?

**Aligned (design with confidence):**
- **WhatsApp-first support & post-purchase updates** — 500M users; the channel *is* the market's customer-service layer. (E1 care lives there.)
- **UPI-ready checkout** — the buyer pays by UPI reflexively; every extra payment step is pure loss. UPI-intent flow is the right next unlock.
- **Mobile-first everything** — sticky ATC, accordions, drawer cart, thumb-zone law: correct for a ~70-75% mobile audience.
- **Video/imagery-led proof** — data is cheap, video consumption is the national default; process transparency in motion fits.
- **Curation over catalog** — quick-commerce conditioning has shortened patience, not increased appetite for browsing; 5 chosen oils is the right shelf.
- **Real-scarcity, no dark patterns** — the 2025 buyer is pattern-literate and the DPDP Act era punishes manipulation; honest scarcity is both safer *and* more persuasive.

**Tensions to manage honestly:**
1. **Attention budget**: reels have trained 1-2 second swipe judgment. Our 50 ms
   aesthetic layer is correct, but the hero must carry meaning *faster* — copy
   must be readable in a glance, not a paragraph.
2. **Discount culture vs. earned premium (E7)**: marketplaces have anchored
   buyers to "deals." The defense is the playbook's: per-day cost framing,
   batch genealogy, and never discounting the brand's confidence. Compete on
   certainty, not price.
3. **Vernacular gap**: a large share of new Indian internet users are
   Indian-language-first (Google/KPMG; IAMAI). Today's storefront is
   English-only — the largest *untapped* alignment lever. Telugu-first
   messaging for the Guntur heartland would out-convert any pixel change.
4. **AI-content skepticism**: AI-generated imagery is now detectable and
   distrusted in food. The brand's real mill footage and real farmer
   stories are *counter-positioning* — use them loudly.
5. **Trust is now cross-platform**: the site is checked against Instagram,
   Google reviews, and WhatsApp reputation before purchase (Stanford 2002
   remains necessary but no longer sufficient — see #3 caveat).

**Update (2026-11-25) — tensions relaxed into executions:** (1) hero now
carries three glance chips + a per-day-cost framing; (2) per-day cost lives
in the PriceBlock and a public `/shop/products/{id}/batch` endpoint feeds
real batch genealogy to the PDP (V23 released batches); (3) Telugu chrome
toggle shipped (ADR-0029) — nav, hero, CTAs, section labels, footer groups;
(4) "Filmed at our Mothadaka mill — real footage, no AI" caption on the hero
plus a no-AI-imagery line in the footer; (5) Find-us row (Instagram ·
YouTube · Google) in the footer. Reviews backend built earlier the same day
(ADR-0028).

**Bottom line:** every research claim in the four documents survives
verification, and the strategy is *native* to the present buyer — the two
highest-value alignment moves (reviews, vernacular) are now built, with the
vernacular slice honestly scoped at chrome level.
