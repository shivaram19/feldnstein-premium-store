## Brand & Style

The brand personality merges agrarian authenticity, premium culinary standards, and clinical ingredient transparency. It serves discerning households seeking unadulterated, farm-verified nutrition, artisanal dairy, and ethically sourced staples. The emotional response is grounded, trustworthy, discerning, and refreshing—evoking the craftsmanship of old-world pastoral heritage paired with uncompromising modern ingredient testing.

The design movement blends **Warm Minimalist Editorial** with **Revealing Tactile Disclosure**:
- Generous, breathable negative space layered over warm, natural surfaces rather than stark digital grays.
- Distinctive architectural card geometry, including arched head-capsules, elongated pill frames, and soft compartmentalized modules inspired by high-end farmstead apothecary and modern direct-to-consumer grocery curation.
- Dual-voice typography that pairs a distinguished, literary editorial serif with a functional, high-clarity humanist sans-serif.
- Structural honesty: rigorous disclosure tags, "what's in vs. what's out" comparative pill badges, and clean traceability indicators.

## Colors

The palette directly honors the brand emblem: deep botanical forest greens, warm harvest ochre gold, and sun-bleached cream grounds.

- **Primary (`#184e32`)**: Botanical Forest Green. Serves as the bedrock of authority, navigation anchors, key interactive states, and authoritative headlines.
- **Secondary (`#c59b27`)**: Rich Mustard / Warm Ochre Gold. Applied for brand emblems, premium highlights, verified certification seals, and tactile focal accents. Supported by a deeper shade (`#b8860b`) for small text and active states.
- **Tertiary (`#0d3822`)**: Deep Forest Shadow. Reserved for heavy contrast anchors, dark conversion blocks, footer containers, and crisp typographic emphasis.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#fcfbf7`)**: Pure Ivory Ground. Forms the default app background, supported by an alternating warm cream container (`#f7f5ee`) and crisp pure white (`#ffffff`) for floating cards and product viewports.
- **Semantic Accents**:
  - Positive / Pure: `#246942` (Herb Green) for non-toxic attributes, clean certifications, and organic labels.
  - Excluded / Alert: `#bc4747` (Terra Rust) for rejected chemicals, banned preservatives, and allergen alerts.
  - Muted Charcoal: `#222522` for standard body typography to ensure soft yet accessible reading contrast.

## Typography

The type scale combines the warmth and editorial tradition of **Newsreader** for headlines with the modern, rounded legibility of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for interfaces, product specs, and transactional labels.

- **Editorial Emphasis**: Headlines often leverage italicized serif words within titles (e.g., *What goes on your plate matters*) to produce an authentic, artisanal magazine voice.
- **Clarity in Data**: Nutrients, lab verification numbers, and ingredient lists use `body-sm` and `label-caps` in Plus Jakarta Sans for crisp, unambiguous scanning.
- **Hierarchy Balance**: Serifs are reserved exclusively for display and section headers; interactive surfaces, form elements, table data, and badges remain strictly sans-serif to preserve scannability.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a 12-column fluid grid on desktop (max container width 1240px) that simplifies to a 6-column grid on tablet (768px–1023px) and a single or 2-column flow on mobile (<768px).

- **Generous Canvas Margins**: Outer canvas margins use `margin` (48px) on desktop to establish an airy, gallery-like catalog presentation, scaling down to `margin-mobile` (20px) on mobile viewports.
- **Section Rhythm**: Generous vertical breathing room (`space-2xl` / 64px) separates informational narrative tiers (e.g., origin story, comparison tables, customer testimonials).
- **Component Padding**: Standard card internal padding conforms to `space-lg` (24px) for desktop and `space-md` (16px) for compact mobile cards.
- **Reflow Behavior**: Multi-card comparison groups (e.g., 3-column pill cards) reflow to horizontal scroll carousels or single-column stacks on screens below 768px.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on warm tonal layering, delicate organic outlines, and minimal, diffused shadows rather than harsh drop shadows.

- **Tonal Layering**: Depth is achieved by placing pure white (`#ffffff`) or light warm-tinted containers (`#f7f5ee`) over the ivory base canvas (`#fcfbf7`). Dark focal blocks (such as the footer or club disclosure modules) adopt Deep Forest (`#0d3822`) to create grounded grounding weights.
- **Ambient Light Shadows**: Elevated interactive elements (such as quick-add carts and floating verification pills) receive an ambient blur: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(24, 78, 50, 0.06)`, introducing a subtle green-tinted organic depth.
- **Hairline Framing**: Structural separation favors ultra-thin warm neutral borders (`1px solid #e9e5d9`) instead of heavy shadows. This keeps product galleries clean and editorial.
- **Glassmorphism for Sticky Headers**: The primary navigation bar uses `background: rgba(252, 251, 247, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and a subtle `border-bottom: 1px solid rgba(197, 155, 39, 0.15)`.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary employs expressive arch-tops, full pill contours, and capsule surfaces that reflect agrarian silos, traditional milk bottles, and organic contours.

- **Arched Feature Cards**: Showcase cards feature an arch-top radius (`border-radius: 999px 999px 24px 24px` on desktop or `120px 120px 20px 20px`), creating an iconic architectural window for featured harvests and dairy curations.
- **Pill Form Factors**: Interactive buttons, disclosure tags, category selectors, and banned-ingredient badges utilize full pill geometry (`rounded-full` / `999px`).
- **Product Tiles**: Everyday product items and secondary content modules use generous softened corners (`rounded-2xl` / 24px) to preserve tactile friendliness.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), background `#184e32`, text `#ffffff`, high-contrast weight `Plus Jakarta Sans 600`. Hover shifts to `#0d3822` with a subtle elevation lift.
- **Accent Gold Action**: Pill-shaped, background `#c59b27`, text `#ffffff`. Used exclusively for direct purchase conversions and membership subscriptions.
- **Secondary Ghost**: Pill-shaped, transparent background, `1.5px solid #184e32`, text `#184e32`. Hover fills with `#184e32` and white text.

### Badges & Comparative Disclosure Pills
- **Approved / Purity Pills**: Full-pill, soft green background (`rgba(24, 78, 50, 0.08)`), text `#184e32`, featuring a verified checkmark icon.
- **Banned / Excluded Badges**: Full-pill, soft salmon background (`rgba(188, 71, 71, 0.08)`), text `#bc4747`, prefixed with a strike-through or cross icon.
- **Ingredient Comparison Cards**: Dual-column contrast panels pairing "Mass Market Standard" against "Our Clean Standard", using arched or pill headers with clear, pill-shaped ingredient tags.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs**: Warm white surface (`#ffffff`), `1px solid #e2ddd0` border, `rounded-full` or `rounded-xl` shape, with inner padding `14px 20px`. Active focus state triggers `border-color: #184e32` with a subtle ring of `rgba(24, 78, 50, 0.12)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Forest green active fills with crisp white checks/dots. Outer ring softly rounded (`6px` for checkboxes, full circle for radios).

### Cards & Grid Containers
- **Architectural Arch Cards**: Solid `#ffffff` background with top-rounded arch framing. Houses high-resolution food photography paired with Newsreader serif headings and Plus Jakarta Sans specs.
- **Category Navigators**: Small rounded capsule modules (`rounded-2xl`) with warm background fills (`#f7f5ee`), centered food photography, and bold micro-labels underneath.
- **Testimonial Modules**: Soft `#ffffff` cards framed in `#ece8dc` featuring 5-star forest-green ratings, quotation marks in Newsreader serif, and circular member avatars.

### Trust Cues & Verification Headers
- **Ticker / Banner**: Running marquee bar with `#f7f5ee` background, ochre dividing dots, and small uppercase labels (`label-caps`) highlighting statements such as "NO SYNTHETIC HORMONES • 100% GRASS FED • LAB TESTED BATCHES".
- **Floating App Scanner / Download Modal**: Crisp white container with arched corners, dark forest green branding footer, and a prominent QR code for instant authentication.
